# LIDEN BROCK
### *The island doesn't want you to leave.*

**A co-op survival horror experience set on a forgotten island where humanity's experiments refuse to stay buried.**

> Four friends. One abandoned facility. Something went terribly wrong.

---

## The Story

A violent storm destroys a yacht, leaving four friends stranded on an uncharted island hidden beneath a dense blanket of fog.

Cut off from civilization, Alexandria, Samuel, Kevin, and Suzie must search the island for the components needed to construct a long-range emergency radio transmitter. Their search leads them to an abandoned bioengineering facility — a place filled with evidence of experiments that were never meant to be discovered.

Something still lives inside.

As resources become scarce and the island's secrets begin to surface, survival becomes more than a race against the elements. Every discovery brings the survivors closer to understanding what happened — and what might happen if they fail to escape.

**The objective is simple: survive, uncover the truth, and find a way home.**

The question is whether the island will let you.

---

## The Experience

Liden Brock is being developed as a **co-op survival horror game with story-driven action and exploration**, built around environmental tension, resource scarcity, and the mystery of a failed bioengineering project.

### Core gameplay pillars

- **Explore the unknown** — Navigate a remote island surrounded by fog, abandoned structures, and unexplained dangers.
- **Scavenge to survive** — Search for components and supplies essential to survival and the construction of an emergency transmitter.
- **Uncover the experiment** — Investigate the abandoned facility and piece together the story behind its secret research.
- **Face the unknown** — Encounter mutated threats and decide when to evade danger and when to fight back.
- **Work together** — Build toward a cooperative experience in which coordination and resource management matter.
- **See the world differently** — Switch between first-person and third-person perspectives for immersive exploration and situational awareness.

## The Gameplay Loop

**Explore → Scavenge → Investigate → Survive → Rebuild**

1. **Explore:** Venture through the island and locate useful resources.
2. **Scavenge:** Collect the components required to repair equipment and construct essential technology.
3. **Investigate:** Discover clues about the abandoned bioengineering facility.
4. **Survive:** Avoid or confront the threats that inhabit the island.
5. **Rebuild:** Progress toward restoring long-range communication and securing a way off the island.

Each stage moves the survivors closer to escape while revealing more of the island's history.

## Project Goals

Our immediate goal is to deliver a cohesive playable prototype rather than a collection of disconnected features.

We are prioritizing:

- A clear gameplay loop with a meaningful objective.
- A distinctive atmosphere built around fog, isolation, and environmental storytelling.
- Responsive player controls and reliable camera switching.
- Modular systems that can be expanded without destabilizing the project.
- A foundation for future cooperative survival gameplay.

The broader vision is to make the island feel like a place with a history — one the player gradually uncovers through exploration rather than exposition alone.
